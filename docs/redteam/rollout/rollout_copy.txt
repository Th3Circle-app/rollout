--- POST (long) ---

Rollout — a real-time 3D music launch platform I built.

An artist drops one finished song and gets a whole release back: AI cover art on a layered canvas, a beat-synced lyric video, a release calendar with pre-written captions, a hosted fan page, and an ad campaign. One platform, start to finish.

The landing is hand-written WebGL and GLSL. A raymarched glass blob that morphs and reacts to your cursor sits behind a real 3D headphones model, with drifting smoke and a star field, all running in a single WebGL context so it holds up across devices instead of fighting for the GPU.

Under the hood it's a real product, not a demo: multi-tenant SaaS on Supabase with Postgres row-level security, Stripe billing with server-enforced plans, and a Python engine that reads the song's key, tempo and mood and turns that reading into the whole visual and marketing direction.

Stack: vanilla JS + WebGL, Supabase, Stripe, Netlify, and a CLAP audio model for the vibe read.
