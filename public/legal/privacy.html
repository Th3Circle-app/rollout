Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Rollout is operated by Th3Circle LLC (California, USA).

This document is a plain-language summary of our practices. It is provided for transparency and should be reviewed by legal counsel before it is relied upon as a binding agreement.

1. Who we are

Rollout is a music-release toolkit operated by Th3Circle LLC ("Rollout", "we", "us"). You can contact us at support@xkaii.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account data — your email address, artist name, and authentication identity (via email or Google sign-in).
  • Content you provide — audio tracks you upload, lyrics, prompts, and the cover art, videos, captions, and release pages generated from them.
  • Usage data — how many songs/generations you've used, plan status, and basic product interactions.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we store only a Stripe customer/subscription ID and your plan.
  • Support messages — anything you send us through the in-app chat or email.
  • Local storage — we store app state and any AI provider key you add only in your browser (see §7).

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: analyze your track and generate your release assets.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To provide support and send service and billing emails (including trial-ending reminders).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your uploads or generated content to train our own AI models.

4. AI usage disclosure

Rollout uses artificial intelligence to generate cover art, videos, captions, and release plans. To do this, your prompts, audio (or an audio-derived embedding), and related inputs are sent to AI providers to produce your assets. AI output can be imperfect or inaccurate, and you are responsible for reviewing it before you publish or distribute it.

6. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. Deletion permanently removes your releases, fan pages, uploaded files, generated assets, and your account identity from our systems. You may also request deletion by emailing support@xkaii.com. We retain limited billing records where required by law.

7. Cookies & local storage

Rollout uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember your workspace, and essential cookies to run the site. We ask for your consent on first visit. We do not run third-party advertising trackers. Any AI provider key you add is stored only in your browser and is never written to our servers.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of it. We do not sell personal data. To exercise any right, email support@xkaii.com.

9. Children

Rollout is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
