How to launch a song in 2026: the full release checklist

A practical run-through of everything a real release needs, and the order to do it in.

Releasing a song is no longer "upload it and hope." In 2026 a release is a small campaign: a set of assets, a schedule, and a few weeks of consistent posting. Here is the checklist we built Rollout around.

1. Lock the song and read its vibe

Before anything visual, get clear on the mood, tempo, and the hook — the 15 seconds people will actually hear first on TikTok and Reels. Everything else (art, video, captions) should match that vibe, not fight it.

2. Cover art that survives a thumbnail

Your cover shows up at 60 pixels wide in a feed. It needs one clear focal point, high contrast, and type that is still legible tiny. Make a few options, then pick the one that reads at thumbnail size, not the one that looks best full screen.

3. A lyric video and a promo clip

Two different jobs. The lyric video is the shareable, full piece for YouTube and your page. The promo clip is a 15–30s vertical teaser cut from the hook, made specifically for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. You want both, and you want the teaser first.

4. Distribution, set up early

Pick a distributor and submit at least 2–3 weeks before your release date so playlists and pre-saves have time to work. Late submissions kill your first-week momentum.

5. A release page and a smart link

One link that sends fans to every platform, plus a simple page that shows the art, the countdown, and the streaming buttons. Put this link in every bio and every post.

6. A posting cadence, not a single announcement

The single biggest mistake is posting once on release day. Instead: tease the hook the week before, post the promo clip on drop day, then keep resurfacing the song with new angles for two to three weeks. Consistency beats a one-time push every time.

7. Measure and repeat

Watch which clip and which caption actually moved saves and streams, then make more of that for the next release. Every drop teaches you something for the next one.
